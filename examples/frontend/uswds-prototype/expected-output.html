About Federal Benefits

Federal benefits programs help eligible individuals and families access financial assistance, healthcare, and other support services. This page will help you understand if you qualify.

Eligibility Requirements

You may be eligible if you:

  • Are a U.S. citizen or qualified non-citizen
  • Meet income requirements for your household size
  • Are a resident of your state
  • Meet any program-specific requirements

How to Apply

  1. Check your eligibility

    Answer a few questions to see if you may qualify for benefits.

  2. Gather your documents

    You will need proof of identity, income, and residence.

  3. Submit your application

    Complete the online application or visit your local office.
